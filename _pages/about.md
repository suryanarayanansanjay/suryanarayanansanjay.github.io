**Hello · नमस्ते · வணக்கம் · ನಮಸ್ಕಾರ !**

I’m Sanjay, a Master’s student in [Computational Linguistics](https://www.compling.uw.edu/) at the University of Washington.

My current research focuses on **Multilingual and Multimodal Language Modeling**. Additionally, I am exploring **Mechanistic Interpretability** and **Reinforcement Learning** as complementary directions within these domains. In simpler terms, I spend my time thinking about how language models work, how to make them better, and why they do things that make absolutely no sense!

Previously, I was a Research Associate at the [AI4Bhārat Lab](https://ai4bharat.iitm.ac.in/) at [IIT Madras](https://www.iitm.ac.in/), where I spent nearly three years researching multilingual and multimodal language modeling and building state-of-the-art neural machine translation (NMT), automatic speech recognition (ASR), and text-to-speech (TTS) models for Indic languages. I had the privilege of working under the guidance of [Dr. Mitesh Khapra](https://www.cse.iitm.ac.in/~miteshk/), [Dr. Raj Dabre](https://prajdabre.github.io/), [Dr. Anoop Kunchukuttan](https://anoopkunchukuttan.github.io/) and [Dr.Rudra Murthy](https://murthyrudra.github.io/).

I speak English, Hindi, Tamil, and Kannada (my brain code-switches more often than my models do!).

Beyond CS research, I’m a [curious soul with wide-ranging interests](/assets/img/meme.jpg). I’m passionate about sports, cars, cinema, and music, and enjoy studying mathematics, physics, economics, neuroscience, philosophy, spirituality, and yes, a decillion other things!

I'm always up for interesting research problems, ambitious ideas, and collaborations with people who are excited about building things that matter. I'm also open to academia and industry research opportunities.

If any of this sounds like your kind of thing, feel free to reach out via [email](mailto:suryanarayanansanjay@gmail.com), [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/suryanarayanansanjay/), or [X](https://x.com/sanjaysury_). I'm always happy to talk research, exchange ideas, or go down an entirely unrelated intellectual rabbit hole!
